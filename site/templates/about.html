About & data

Launch Delay Watch is an open-source dataset and site that tracks how rocket launch NET dates move over time.

Attribution

Launch records come from Launch Library 2 by The Space Devs. Please attribute them when you reuse launch metadata. This site only reads committed CSV snapshots — it does not call the API at build time.
