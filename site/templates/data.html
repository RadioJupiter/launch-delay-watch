Columns and method

Each snapshot is one request to the Launch Library 2 launch/upcoming endpoint (next 100 launches). slip_days is the newer NET minus the older NET, in days (+ = later). precision_flag is ok only when both NETs are given to the hour or better; precision_change and low_precision rows (day, month, quarter or year placeholders such as 2026-11-30T00:00:00Z) are excluded from slip statistics. The full schema and limitations are in the README.
